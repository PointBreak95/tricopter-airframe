# 2026-11-10 — Baseline and setup

**Phase:** 1 – Requirements

## Done
- Inventoried the parts salvaged from the scrapped first tricopter: 3x EMAX ECO II 2807 1500KV motors, 3x XRotor 40A ESCs, MG90S servo, RadioMaster R88 receiver, RadioMaster Pocket (ELRS) transmitter, 608AC charger, Ovonic 4S 2200 mAh and 3S 1300 mAh LiPos, Pixhawk 2.4.8, Matek PDB.
- Inventoried tools: Bambu A1 mini (PLA, 180 mm bed), calipers, soldering iron, digital kitchen scale, threaded inserts and screws.
- Recorded the first frame's failure history: never flew; split along layer lines at the arm-to-hub joints; transmitter never bound to a receiver.

## Decisions
- Stay with a tricopter configuration and reuse the salvaged parts.
- Budget ceiling: $300 of additional spend.
- Modular, interchangeable arms on threaded inserts instead of interlocking printed joints.

## Open
- Shear vs. bending at the arm root.
- Component weights, arm dimensions from the old CAD, which LiPo has the charging issue, radio protocol check.
